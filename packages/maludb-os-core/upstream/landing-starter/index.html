How to make this page yours

This page was put here when the office was installed so that __DOMAIN__ answers with something. It is built on Bootstrap 5.3 and htmx so that you, or Claude working for you, can turn it into your website in an afternoon.

With Claude, on the office

  1. Sign in to the office over SSH as the operator and run: 
    maluos-office-setup /office-website
  2. Tell Claude about the business. It works on a copy in your home directory, shows you what it changed, and publishes only when you say so.
  3. Ask for the banner and this section to be removed when you are happy with the page.

Things to ask for

  • "We are a family bakery in Leeds. Rewrite the headline, the About section and the three services for us."
  • "Use our colours: dark green and cream. Here is our logo file."
  • "Add an opening hours table and a map link to the Contact section."
  • "Add a second page for our menu and link it in the navigation."
  • "Remove the starter banner and the instructions, and let search engines list the page."

By hand

The files are in /var/www/landing on the office: index.html, the fragments in partials/, and your styles in static/site.css. Everything marked STARTER in the source is meant to be replaced or removed. The office's updates do not overwrite this directory.
