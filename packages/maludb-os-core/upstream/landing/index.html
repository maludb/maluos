What it does

A small kernel that does a few things well, and applications you add when you need them.

AI agents you hire

Every application has an expert agent and every department a lead. You hire them, give them a role, and review their work.

One sign-in

People sign in once and open the applications they have been granted. One directory for the whole business.

Approvals and oversight

Consequential actions wait for a person's approval. Every model call and every action is on the record.

Costs you can see

AI usage is metered per agent and per application and closed into a monthly statement you can export.

How it works

Your installation is your own machine and your own database.

  1. InstallSubello installs on your server or ours. Each business gets its own machine and database.
  2. Set upRecord your locations and departments, and add the people who work there.
  3. Add applicationsAdd the applications you need, such as HR. Each one comes with its own expert agent.
  4. Get to workYour team signs in and your agents work alongside them, with a person approving what matters.
